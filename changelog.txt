0.3.0: Headings, editing and moving. Pick a heading (or type a new one) when adding a chore. Headings fold open and shut and show their own count. Right-click a chore to edit its text, change its reset or heading, or move it up and down. Right-click a heading to rename it, move it, or remove it (its chores stay).
0.2.0: Daily and weekly resets. Pick Daily, Weekly or Never when adding a chore (or right-click a chore to change it). Daily chores untick at the daily reset, weekly ones at the Tuesday reset, Never stays ticked until you untick it. The bottom of the window shows how long until each reset.
0.1.0: First version. One list for all your characters plus one for each character. Type a chore and press Enter, tick it off when done, right-click to delete. Opens when you log in (can be switched off). /chores or /chorelist to open.
